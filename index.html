02 / HOW I GOT HERE

A journey shaped
by curiosity.

Each step added a new lens: how people work, how systems connect, why data matters, and what quality means to the person using a product.

2016

While studying English and International Affairs, I joined a textile manufacturer in Mexico as a management intern and assistant to the General Manager. I supported the introduction of an ERP system — helping with processes, user training, rollout and troubleshooting.

That experience sparked my interest in how technology can change the way people work.

2018—22

I moved to Germany to study International Information Systems. Collaborating with a technology startup supporting women broadened my view of technology: it is not only about systems, but also about the people whose everyday lives those systems can improve.

I became interested in the connection between technology, people and practical outcomes.

2020

At PUMA's headquarters, I worked in Master Data Management and supported the Product Information Management system, data validation and workflow improvements.

I learned that reliable data takes real work — and that it is foundational to reliable systems.

2021—22

I worked with information-security master data, audited data quality, resolved cross-system gaps and supported governance, risk and compliance activities.

This strengthened my appreciation for data integrity, traceability and clear coordination across teams.

2022—26

As a Quality Engineer and project coordinator, I worked across frontend and backend automation, complex data pipelines, release quality and cross-functional initiatives. I also took ownership of an accessibility initiative spanning multiple development teams.

I learned to look beyond whether something works — and ask whether it works well for the people who depend on it.
